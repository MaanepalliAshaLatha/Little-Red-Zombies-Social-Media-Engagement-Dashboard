🎮 Little Red Zombies — Social Media Engagement Dashboard<br><br>
Tools: Power BI, Google Sheets<br>
Data: 100 manually collected data points across 4 platforms — ArtStation, LinkedIn, Instagram, X<br><br>
📌 Business Problem<br>
Little Red Zombies is a world-class AAA 3D character art studio working with PlayStation, Xbox, Ubisoft and 17 other global publishers. Despite strong industry credibility, their social media presence does not reflect their scale or quality of work.
This analysis identifies which platforms are performing, where engagement is being lost, and what content and platform strategy changes can improve visibility and reach.<br><br>
❓ Key Questions
<br>
Which platform has the highest engagement rate and why?
Where is LRZ underutilising their social presence?
What type of content drives the most engagement?
Which platforms need immediate action?
<br><br>
📊 Key Insights
<br>
ArtStation leads with 26,200 followers but has been inactive for 10 months — significant missed opportunity on their strongest platform
Instagram has the highest engagement rate (4.97%) despite the smallest audience (3,133 followers) — most underutilised platform
Iconic character artwork (Half-Life Gordon — 8,600 likes) drives 10x higher engagement than technical asset posts
X is effectively dead — 116 followers, 15 original posts, last activity May 2024
<br><br>
🧩 Platform Segmentation<br>
🟢 High Potential: Instagram — highest engagement rate, Reels reach non-followers, massive growth opportunity<br>
🟡 Stable but Declining: LinkedIn — moderate engagement, consistent B2B credibility but declining trend in early 2026<br>
🔴 Needs Immediate Action: ArtStation — strongest platform but inactive. X — no meaningful reach or ROI<br><br>
💼 Recommendations
<br>
Reactivate ArtStation immediately — post minimum twice a month leading with game titles not asset types
Invest in Instagram Reels — reaches non-followers, highest growth potential for new client discovery
Either revive X with a clear strategy or redirect that effort to Instagram and LinkedIn
Lead every post with the game name — "We built characters for Split Fiction" connects more than "Hair simulation project"
Mention client names in every project post — 20 world-class clients are an underutilised credibility asset
<br><br>
📈 Approach
<br>
Researched LRZ's industry positioning, clients, services and achievements before data collection<br>
Manually collected 100 data points from public profiles across 4 platforms as of May 2026<br>
Calculated engagement rates using formula: (Avg Likes + Avg Comments) ÷ Followers × 100<br>
Built two-page interactive Power BI dashboard with KPI cards, trend analysis and platform comparison visuals<br>
Delivered structured report covering approach, insights, recommendations and assumptions<br>

⚠️ Assumptions & Limitations
<br>
Instagram video average (206 likes) based on only 2 data points — may not be representative<br>
X engagement rate (4.53%) is statistically insignificant at 116 followers — excluded from meaningful platform comparison<br>
LinkedIn post dates approximated from relative timestamps where exact dates were not visible<br>
ArtStation older artworks (2018–2020) have higher engagement partially due to longer accumulation time<br>
